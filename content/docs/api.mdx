# Trust, data, and approvals

OtterDesk is designed to make supervision visible. A co-worker should have a clear mission, understandable activity, and a defined limit on what it can do without you.

## Review access before use

Before you hire or configure a co-worker, inspect:

- What you provide.
- What it returns.
- The capabilities and requirements listed on its detail page.
- Its connected data source or integration, if any.
- Its stated limitations and approval needs.

## Approval boundaries

When a co-worker needs a decision, OtterDesk shows a **Decision needed** card with the choices available. It waits for your approval, rejection, revision, or reply before continuing.

## Evidence first

For an important recommendation, open the evidence attached to it. Use the co-worker's review view to understand what it observed and why it reached the result.

For data handling and local controls, read [Privacy and safety](/docs/security).
