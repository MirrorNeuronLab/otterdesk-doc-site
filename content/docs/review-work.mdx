Open the co-worker in Chat and begin with the newest factual update. Check the result, its source material, and any request waiting for you.

## A useful review sequence

1. Confirm the mission and inputs match the task you intended.
2. Read the latest result and its limitations.
3. Open its evidence or full report when you need to verify a claim.
4. Check requested input in **Respond** and record your decision there.
5. Decide whether to keep the settings, correct them, run again, or pause future work.

## Reports and media in Chat

Report presentation depends on what the co-worker publishes:

- A saved text report can appear directly in the conversation.
- A summary can offer **View full report**, opening an interactive report in the shared viewer.
- Static web reports can appear inline with **Show full report**.
- Evidence can include image thumbnails or MP4/WebM clips. Video uses explicit playback controls; it does not autoplay.
- Monitoring observations can show a short excerpt, local capture time, and a thumbnail, with details available in place.

**Discuss findings** and similar actions fill a Chat draft. They do not send it or record approval. Report controls inspect saved output; use the co-worker's declared review controls for decisions. Specialized report workspaces may have their own save/export steps for retaining wording reviews.

Saved conversation previews can be reopened after restart. Original reports remain in the run's output folder. Opening an older report does not make it evidence of the current run.

## Follow steps and activity

Select **View details → Status** for the run assignment and workflow. **List** helps you scan steps; **Graph** shows their relationships. Select a step to focus its activity. Runtime events lead with the work message; expand event details when you need more context.

The assigned computer and reserved CPU, RAM, disk, and GPU amounts are separate from measured utilization. Queued work can have a destination without a reservation. Missing measurements remain unavailable.

Use **Inputs & outputs**, the workspace **Files** view, and **Logs** for deeper investigation. These views help inspect work; they do not certify correctness.

## Respond to a decision

A review request can restrict the next step until you answer or it expires. Read its exact scope, choices, and evidence, then use **Respond**. Choosing a report tab, discussing a finding, or reading an explanation is not an approval. See [Approvals and requested input](/docs/approvals).
