---
title: Account and credits
description: Use free co-workers without Google sign-in and understand account-gated actions.
---

OtterDesk opens directly into the app. Google sign-in is optional for free local co-workers; account features and paid roles can require it.

## Sign in or out

Select **Sign in** in the sidebar. Google authentication opens your system browser; return to OtterDesk after completing it. A saved Google session stays connected until you sign out or access is revoked.

The app can create an anonymous account in the background when the service is reachable. Background sign-up does not block local work or require you to finish a sign-in dialog. Without a connection, free local work remains available when its runtime, models, and inputs are already prepared.

Selecting your connected account opens its balance, website profile link, and sign-out action. Signing out does not delete the co-workers or local data on this computer. Signing in is not a cloud backup or synchronization of your team to another computer.

## Paid access

Check the catalog's credit notice and the current account terms before using a paid role. In the current app, **Software Architecture** requires Google sign-in and a daily credit entitlement. Hiring and configuration do not spend credits. The first authorized start, resume, retry, or knowledge Chat for a UTC day activates that day's access; later attempts that day are included, including after a runtime failure.

Paid actions require a reachable account authorization service. An account outage, expired session, and insufficient credits are different from runtime capacity or setup failures.

When **Sign in to continue** appears, completing sign-in closes the dialog. Retry the original action yourself; sign-in does not automatically hire or start work.

## Balance, purchases, and likes

Use **Settings → General** to refresh the shared balance. **Buy credits on the website** opens the purchase flow; the desktop does not collect payment details. Use the same Google account on the website and app, and review the current package and checkout terms there.

A catalog **Like** can ask for Google sign-in before saving a vote. It does not hire the co-worker or authorize a run.
