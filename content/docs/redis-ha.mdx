# When work needs your attention

OtterDesk makes attention visible so you can decide, correct, or pause without searching through technical details.

## Decision needed

A **Decision needed** card means the co-worker is waiting for you. Read the prompt, add notes if helpful, then choose the available action:

- **Approve** to allow the proposed next step.
- **Reject** to stop that proposal.
- **Revise** to ask for changes.
- Another named option when the co-worker needs a more specific choice.
- **Send reply** to provide a direct response in your own words.

The co-worker does not continue past that request until you respond.

## Missed shifts or schedule errors

Open **Shifts** and refresh the schedule state. Check whether the schedule is enabled and whether the runtime is healthy. If an error remains, open the co-worker's review view and see [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).

## A result you do not trust

Do not act on it yet. Open the evidence, check the inputs, clarify the co-worker's configuration, and run a focused test. You can also pause the schedule while you investigate.
