OtterDesk is a desktop app for supervising AI co-workers on computers you control. Choose a mission, give a co-worker the inputs it needs, and review its work before making a decision.

Start with one co-worker and a small, reviewable task. Free co-workers do not require Google sign-in.

## Get your first result

1. [Install OtterDesk](/docs/installation) and let it prepare the runtime.
2. [Hire a co-worker](/docs/hire-a-co-worker) whose mission fits your task.
3. [Complete setup in Chat](/docs/setup). Choose sample data when offered, or supply your own inputs. **The final required setup answer can start work automatically.**
4. [Review the result and its evidence](/docs/review-work), then correct the inputs or [set a schedule](/docs/schedules).

Follow [Quick start](/docs/quickstart) for the complete walkthrough.

## Stay in control

A co-worker's mission, connected inputs, limitations, and approval requirements matter more than its name or status badge. Review those details before giving it real data. Local execution does not guarantee that every configured integration stays offline: see [Privacy and safety](/docs/privacy-and-safety).

These guides describe the current desktop interface. Available catalog roles, reports, integrations, and hardware requirements depend on the co-worker and the installed runtime. Screenshots use demonstration data; they show the interface, not measured production results.
