# Preferences and local data

Open **Settings** from the sidebar to manage the choices that affect how OtterDesk feels and behaves on this computer.

## General

- Choose whether OtterDesk starts when you sign in.
- Choose Light, Dark, or your system theme.
- Turn on optional test substitutes for **Test now**.

## Archived

Review co-workers you have archived. Their local history remains available until you permanently remove them.

## Resources

Check local or cluster capacity, add a trusted computer, and review runtime health.

## Updates

Choose whether OtterDesk checks for updates and whether eligible runtime components can update automatically.

These settings are intended for normal supervision. If a setting affects a co-worker's mission, configure that co-worker directly from **My Team** instead.
