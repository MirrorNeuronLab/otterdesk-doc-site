---
title: Connect co-workers around a goal
description: Build a collaboration board with compatible co-workers and review communication changes before saving.
---

Open **Collaboration** in the sidebar to connect compatible co-workers around a shared goal. A board configures communication; it does not start runs or grant approvals.

## Prepare the members

Hire the co-workers first. Stop their work and disable their schedules before changing communication. The catalog determines which co-workers are compatible and the supported group size. Every member must be compatible with every other member, and a co-worker can belong to one board.

## Create or edit a board

1. Open **Collaboration** and select **Enable** to edit.
2. Use **Add co-workers**, or drag eligible co-workers from My Team onto the board.
3. Select **Change goal**, write the shared goal, then **Apply goal** to update the draft.
4. Position member cards so the group is easy to inspect.
5. Select **Save changes** to apply communication settings.

You can create up to five boards with **+**. Rename a tab from its menu, by double-clicking it, or with F2. Member cards share the board's goal; you do not need connection lines or a goal node.

## Drafts, saves, and partial results

Tab switching and navigation preserve unsaved drafts for the current app session. **Cancel** returns to the saved board. Saved names and layouts survive restart.

If a save only partly succeeds, the board shows **Needs review**. Inspect confirmed changes and use its setup/retry actions. Do not assume that every member's communication changed together.

## Remove a board

Removing a saved board asks for confirmation and disconnects its members. The co-workers remain in My Team. Removing a draft addition releases its reservation; saved membership stays reserved until disconnection is confirmed.

After saving, start co-workers through their normal controls and keep reviewing each one's evidence and requests. See [Run controls](/docs/run-and-pause) and [Approvals](/docs/approvals).
