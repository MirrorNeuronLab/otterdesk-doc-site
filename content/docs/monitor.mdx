# Review work

Review work is how you stay informed without following every implementation detail. Open a co-worker from **My Team**, then use its status and activity views to understand what changed and whether it needs you.

## What to look for

- **Current status:** whether the co-worker is ready, running, scheduled, paused, or needs review.
- **Recent activity:** the latest observations and steps.
- **Evidence:** the supporting details attached to a result or decision.
- **Inputs and outputs:** what the co-worker received and produced.
- **Decision needed:** a prompt that requires your approval, rejection, revision, or reply.

## A practical review routine

1. Start with the mission and current status.
2. Read the newest activity and result.
3. Open evidence when you need to understand why the result was reached.
4. Respond to any decision card.
5. Choose the next action: keep the schedule, correct configuration, test again, pause, or archive.

For workflow steps and progress views, see [Work progress and steps](/docs/dag-flow-patterns). For status definitions, see [Understand co-worker status](/docs/reliability).
