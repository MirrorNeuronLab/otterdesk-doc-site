# Co-worker knowledge and workspace

A co-worker may use a workspace to organize the information relevant to its mission. OtterDesk presents this as part of the co-worker's context, not as a general-purpose file manager.

## What you can review

Depending on the co-worker, you may see:

- Recent work and observations.
- Helpful knowledge or saved context.
- A workspace folder.
- Evidence connected to a particular result.

## Keep the scope intentional

Use a workspace only for materials appropriate to the co-worker's mission. Before you add a connection or change access, review the co-worker's stated data boundaries in its detail or settings views.

OtterDesk keeps the focus on the outcome and evidence. It does not expose raw internal context in normal review screens.

For broader data and control guidance, see [Trust, data, and approvals](/docs/api) and [Privacy and safety](/docs/security).
