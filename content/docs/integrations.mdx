---
title: Use Slack with a co-worker
description: Connect a supported co-worker to a Slack channel and inspect its route.
---

For eligible co-workers, the **Integration** tab provides Slack connection and channel routing. Check the co-worker's support and response-service readiness before relying on a route.

## Connect a channel

1. Open the co-worker's **View details → Integration** tab.
2. Use the Slack connection controls to connect a workspace when needed.
3. Create a channel or choose an existing channel using the available route controls.
4. Save the route and inspect the resulting workspace/channel mapping.

Keep channels limited to material appropriate for the co-worker's mission. Connecting a channel sends its routed messages to that co-worker; review access with the workspace owner when necessary.

## What Slack replies do

Slack is a separate reply surface with narrower authority than desktop Chat. It does not provide the desktop's run-start confirmations, lifecycle tools, or configuration proposals. Use OtterDesk to start or pause work, change settings, and handle formal reviews.

If replies are unavailable, check the route, connected workspace, and the co-worker's response readiness. Do not infer that a Slack message started work.

## Change or remove a route

Review routes when a role changes. Remove a mapping when the co-worker should no longer receive that channel's messages. Disconnect unused workspace access through the integration controls.

For a client that needs direct co-worker tools, see [Connect an external AI tool](/docs/external-ai). That connection grants different capabilities and requires separate authorization.
