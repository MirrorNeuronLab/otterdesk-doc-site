A useful hire starts with a clear task and an output you can review. Select **Hire a co-worker** in the sidebar, then search or filter by category.

<AppScreenshot src="/images/app/hire-catalog.png" alt="Sample catalog cards with search, category filters, hardware notices, and hiring controls." caption="Each catalog entry offers View details before you hire." />

## Inspect the role

Select **View details** and check the information supplied by the catalog:

| Detail | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Mission and **What to expect** | Does this role solve the task you actually have? |
| **You provide** | Do you have the required input, permission to use it, and any necessary credentials? |
| **You receive** and **Example result** | Will the result support a decision you can review? |
| **How it works** and **Skills used** | Are its approach and dependencies appropriate for the task? |
| **Requirements** | Can a healthy connected computer meet the specific CPU, memory, GPU, and platform needs? |
| **Trust profile** and terms | Which data can it access, what are its limits, and which actions require you? |
| Credits | Does this role require an account or paid access? |

If a field says **No data**, treat it as missing information. It is not evidence of unrestricted support or a safety guarantee. An **Air-gapped** badge appears only when the catalog explicitly declares it.

## Hire and open

1. Select **Hire** on a card or **Hire co-worker** on its detail page.
2. Follow the hiring progress. You can use other parts of the app while it prepares the co-worker.
3. Wait for the confirmation, then choose **Open conversation**, or select the co-worker in My Team.

OtterDesk checks hardware requirements before committing the hire. A failure should leave you with a concrete requirement or runtime error to resolve. See [Resources](/docs/resources) if no suitable computer is available.

## What happens next

Chat introduces the new co-worker and collects required setup. Choosing sample data or answering the final required question can start work. To inspect or edit settings without starting, use **View details → Configure**.

Catalog roles can differ in setup, reports, tools, and integrations. Follow the selected co-worker's instructions instead of assuming every example in this guide is available to every role.
