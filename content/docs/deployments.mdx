# Archive, remove, and update

When a co-worker is no longer part of your active team, archive it first. Archiving removes it from **My Team** but keeps its local configuration, logs, and records available for review.

## Archive a co-worker

Open the co-worker and choose **Archive**. Use this when you may need to inspect or restore the historical record later.

Archived co-workers appear under **Settings → Archived**. From there, you can review available history or permanently remove a co-worker when you are sure it is no longer needed.

## Permanently remove a co-worker

Permanent removal is destructive. Confirm that you no longer need the co-worker's local configuration, credentials, and run history before you choose the permanent delete action.

## Updates

In **Settings → Updates**, you can choose whether OtterDesk checks for updates and whether it updates eligible co-worker runtime components. Automatic checks look for updates in the background; they do not install OtterDesk automatically.

Keep OtterDesk and the local runtime current before investigating a persistent issue.
