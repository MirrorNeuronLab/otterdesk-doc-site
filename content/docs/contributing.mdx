# Get help and share feedback

When you contact support or share feedback, describe the user-facing problem in plain language:

- What you were trying to do.
- Which co-worker was involved.
- What you expected to happen.
- What you saw instead.
- The visible status or safe error message.

For a reproducible issue, include the steps that led to it. You can also say whether the problem happens during **Test now**, a scheduled shift, chat, configuration, or connection to another PC.

Never include passwords, access tokens, federation tokens, or private source material.

Before reaching out, check [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) and [Check your setup](/docs/services-and-health-checks).
